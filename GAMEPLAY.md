# Gameplay pillars

## The Kor Dynasty
The Kor are the primary playable faction. Their identity is a community that grows through logistics, research, protection and collective resilience. The visual palette is crimson, black and gold.

## Core loop
1. Establish the command core and keep Pioneers alive.
2. Expand the resource economy with Solar Collectors, Extractors and Knowledge Archives.
3. Use research to improve the settlement and unlock later strategic systems.
4. Defend the perimeter from the Blight, whose entities attack Kor forces and spread over time.
5. Continue expanding instead of ending at a fixed campaign victory screen.

## Current prototype
The Unity build uses generated terrain and primitive meshes so that the game systems can be iterated without waiting for final art. Resource counters, construction orders, research, movement and simple combat are implemented in the runtime controller.

## Next milestones
- Construction times and actual Pioneer work assignments.
- A* pathfinding, drag selection, attack-move, unit formations and line-of-sight.
- Seed selection and world presets; larger streamed maps.
- Persistent save/load and deterministic simulation state.
- Multi-branch research with prerequisites and meaningful unlocks.
- Blight ecology with nests, spread pressure, evolving threats and countermeasures.
- Production-ready Kor models, animation, audio, UI and Linux player builds.
- A deeper 4X layer: territory, logistics, settlement policies, diplomacy and long-term world events.
